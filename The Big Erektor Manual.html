Overview

The Erektor is a modular heavy industrial robot that transports raw materials around the factory floor, holds and assembles the aluminum frame of a commercial EV charging station, self-loads the finished station onto a flatbed trailer, self-unloads by walking off the trailer edge, and places it gently at its destination. The robot and the product it builds share a structural skeleton — the charging station's own aluminum rail becomes the Erektor's rigid chassis once legs bolt on.

Powering on legs and connecting to a controiller
  • Fully independent, self-contained — own power, control, radio
  • 3 caster wheels: 2 free-spin, 1 fixed to the drive-axis motor
  • 2 motors per leg — drive axis (foot wheel) and lift axis (ground clearance, trailer transition)
  • Teknic ClearPath-SC servos, one ClearCore board per leg
  • 24V logic/control, 56V direct to motors, one XBee radio per leg

Module & build
  • Module = one right leg + one left leg — logical grouping only, no shared structure
  • Builds use 3–6 modules (6–12 legs) depending on charging-station length
  • Pairing is arbitrary each session — no persistent "Erektor" identity
  • Legs bolt to a universal mounting bracket, fixed attachment points
